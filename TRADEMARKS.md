## What requires a different name and logo

Forks are welcome. However, if you distribute a **modified** version (a fork, a rebuild with changes, or a repackaged installer), you must:

1. Rename it, so that it is not called "Billie" or anything confusingly similar.
2. Replace the files in `frontend/public/brand/` and `src-tauri/icons/` with your own.
3. Change `productName` and `identifier` in `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json`.

You also may not:

- Suggest that your project is the official Billie or is endorsed by its author
- Use the brand in a product name, domain name, app store listing or social media account in a way that could be confused with Billie
- Alter the logo (colors, proportions, rotation) and present it as the Billie logo
